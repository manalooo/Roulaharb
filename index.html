About the Artist

Born in Paris.
Rooted in Beirut.

Roula Harb is a creative designer with over 15 years of experience in fashion and sales. Born in Paris and based in Beirut, she draws inspiration from modern painters — Jackson Pollock, Cy Twombly — and fashion icons including Rei Kawakubo and Manish Arora, alongside master makeup artist Elise Faas.

After the August 4th explosion that devastated Beirut, Roula witnessed catastrophic tragedy — both in her city and in her own life. Seeking healing, she turned to color and art. What emerged was a powerful creative practice: painting as a path to sanity amid chaos.

This journey gave birth to Jookh Couture — exquisite wearable art — and P-Lo, a brand of hand-painted, hand-embroidered pillows. Each piece is a testament to resilience, beauty, and the transformative power of creation.

— Roula Harb
